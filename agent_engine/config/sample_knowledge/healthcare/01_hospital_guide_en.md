Visiting hours
Visiting hours at Sample City Hospital are from 4 pm to 7 pm every day. Each patient may have two visitors at a time. Children under 12 years may not visit the intensive care unit. Visitors must carry a visitor pass from the reception desk.

Departments and services
Sample City Hospital has departments of cardiology, orthopaedics, paediatrics, general medicine, gynaecology, dermatology, radiology and physiotherapy. The pathology laboratory is on the ground floor. The pharmacy is on the ground floor near the main entrance and is open 24 hours a day.

Outpatient timings
The outpatient department is open from Monday to Saturday, from 9 am to 5 pm. It is closed on Sundays and national holidays.

How to book an appointment
To book an appointment, call the appointment desk on 040 5550 1234 between 9 am and 5 pm, or visit the reception desk in person. Please bring a photo identity card and any earlier medical reports to your appointment. New patients should arrive 20 minutes early to complete registration.

Billing and insurance
The billing counter is next to the main reception and is open from 8 am to 8 pm. The hospital accepts cash, debit cards, credit cards and UPI. For cashless insurance, take your insurance card and photo identity card to the insurance desk at least one day before a planned admission.

Getting here
The hospital is on Lake Road, opposite the Central Bus Station. Parking is available in the basement. A wheelchair is available free of charge at the main entrance.

Emergency department
The emergency department is open 24 hours a day and has a separate entrance on the east side of the building.
